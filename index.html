Our Footprint

Nonstop Operations
Across Libya Since 2007

From Tripoli and Benghazi to field locations across Libya’s producing regions, our infrastructure supports the mobilisation of people, equipment and technical resources.

Since 2007, we have maintained our people and operational presence in Libya through the 2011 conflict, subsequent instability and the COVID-19 pandemic. Where conditions affected project schedules, our teams remained alongside clients and continued working towards completion.
