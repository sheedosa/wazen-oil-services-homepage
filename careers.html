Building the Workforce Behind the Infrastructure

Professional Development

Forging Careers on the Frontlines of Energy

Developing a highly capable local workforce is central to our operational strategy. Whether driving strategic project management and logistics from Wazen HQ or executing complex technical operations in the field, we advance our employees’ careers by putting them at the centre of the challenges that directly impact Libya’s daily oil and gas output.

A Culture of Safe Execution

We prepare our teams to execute at the highest level. From engineering to logistics, our workforce operates within rigorous systems and processes that meet global HSE and technical standards. This framework ensures every project is delivered safely, protecting our frontline personnel while providing our entire team with the operational support they need to perform at their best.

Practical, High-Impact Work

We believe true expertise is built in the field through real-world experience. We immerse both our corporate and field teams directly into the operational realities of the energy sector. Whether you are driving project management from our headquarters or delivering technical solutions on active worksites, your work directly drives the operations that sustain Libya’s oil and gas production.

Advancing Libyan Talent

Wazen is committed to advancing the capability of the local workforce. Through our longstanding internship programme, we integrate recent graduates from the Petroleum Training Institute into a practical rotation across our active operations. Those who begin their careers at Wazen receive structured mentorship and on-the-ground opportunities to accelerate their professional growth.
